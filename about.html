About

Why we made A Little Light.

A phone is easy to pick up and hard to put down, especially late at night. We wanted something that meets that moment with a few words of Torah, gently, and then trusts you.

Our name

From the Tanya.

The Tanya teaches that a little light pushes away a great deal of darkness, and the darkness gives way by itself. That is the whole idea: a few words of Torah, right before the app opens. Read the passage.

What matters to us

Made with care.

Your privacy

The app cannot use the internet. There is no account and no tracking, your settings stay on your phone, and backups are off.

Torah, handled carefully

The Hebrew comes from editions on Sefaria, and every passage shows where it is from. Names of Hashem are shown respectfully in the app, and this site shows none.

Your choice

Making things stricter works right away. Easing off waits ten minutes and asks you to confirm, and you can change your mind.

Plain about what it can do

Android lets you uninstall any app, this one included, and the app tells you so.
