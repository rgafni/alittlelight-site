How it works

Four quiet steps.

  1. You choose the apps

    For each app you pick one of three: Open, Pause or Block. Pause is the recommended one. Nothing is chosen for you.

  2. A passage appears first

    When you open an app or site set to Pause, a full screen of Torah comes up before it. It can be one line or a longer passage that scrolls verse by verse.

  3. A short reading pause

    Continue counts down before it can be pressed. The pause is 3, 5, 8 or 10 seconds, and 8 is the default. Go back is always the strongest button on the screen.

  4. Then it is your choice

    Go back takes you home. Another pasuk shows a different passage. Continue opens the app for 15 minutes by day or 5 minutes at night, and you can change both, along with the night hours (10 PM to 6 AM to start).

When a Continue runs out, the pause comes back. Block keeps an app closed.

What you see

Calm screens, made for a late hour.

The app is designed for the hour when the scrolling has already gone on too long. It follows your phone's light or dark setting, and the dark version is deep navy with warm, dim amber, never a bright glare.

These are drawings made for this page, not screenshots, and the app names are examples.

What you read

Real Torah, from Sefaria.

The app carries hundreds of short passages, with the Hebrew and a plain English translation. Every passage has its source, and its edition details one tap away.

You choose the families you want, and all of them start on.

  • Tanach
  • Mishna and Gemara
  • Machshava
  • Chassidus
  • Emuna
  • Halacha
  • Hebrew, English, or both. Pick what you want to see, and watch a live example change as you do.
  • Star what speaks to you. Keep your own groups, with one level of subgroups. Deleting a group never removes a star.
  • My line is optional. If you wrote a line for yourself, it appears with the passage. If not, you see only Torah.
  • Divine Names are handled with care. The app has its own way of displaying them respectfully, and this page shows none.

Your choice

It never fights you.

A Little Light is the inspiration layer, not a filter and not a lock. It is meant to sit alongside the filter and the guidance you already trust, not replace them.

  • Tightening is instant. Move an app from Open to Pause, or to Block, and it takes effect right away.
  • Loosening waits ten minutes. Turning the guard off, or loosening an app, waits ten minutes, then asks you to confirm. You can cancel the request.
  • Honest about its limits. Android controls uninstalling and permissions, and the app does not try to prevent either. It says so plainly.

Privacy

Built to stay on your phone.

  • No internet permission. The app does not ask Android for it, so nothing you do in the app can leave the phone through it. The passages are stored inside the app.
  • No account, no analytics. Your settings stay on your phone, and backups are off.
  • It sees which app is in front. In a browser it also sees the web address. It uses Android's accessibility access for this, and it needs window-content access so your website rules can work.
