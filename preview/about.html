About

Built around one honest idea.

The phone pulls hardest when we are tired and alone. A Little Light answers that pull with Torah instead of a lock, and then it trusts you.

Why it exists

The inspiration layer.

Filters do their job well, and A Little Light does not compete with them. Its job is different: at the moment you reach for an app, it puts a passage of Torah in front of you and gives you a breath to read it. What happens next is your decision.

Every part of the app is measured against one scene: one in the morning, in bed, in a dark room. If the app does not put something beautiful in front of you there, quietly and without glare, it has failed. That is why it follows your phone's dark setting and glows in warm amber on deep navy.

How we work

Promises we keep.

We never claim power we do not have.

Android controls uninstalling and permissions. The app does not try to stop either, and it says so on its own screen: this is not a lock.

Privacy is built in, not written in.

The app does not ask Android for internet access. There is no account and no analytics, your settings stay on your phone, and backups are off.

Friction only when you choose it.

Making a rule stricter happens right away. Making it looser waits ten minutes and then asks you to confirm, and you can cancel the request.

Torah, handled with care.

The Hebrew comes from editions on Sefaria, and each passage shows its source and edition details. Divine Names are displayed respectfully in the app, and this site shows none.

What we leave out

Some things will never be in it.

A calm app is mostly made of what it refuses to add.

  • Streaks
  • Scores
  • Badges
  • Counts of failures
  • Mascots
  • Accounts
  • Analytics
  • Cloud
