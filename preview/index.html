מְעַט אוֹר

A little light at the moment of choice.

Our mission is to meet the pull of the phone with Torah, right at the moment of choice, and to leave the choice with you.

For the late hours

1:00

It is one in the morning. You are in bed, the room is dark, and the scrolling has gone on longer than you wanted.

Before the next app opens, A Little Light shows you a few lines of Torah, softly lit and easy on tired eyes. Then you decide what comes next.

How it works

A pause, a passage, then your choice.

  1. Pick the apps that pull at you

    For each app, you decide: open as usual, a moment of Torah first, or closed.

  2. Torah comes first

    When you open one of those apps, a passage of Torah fills the screen before it, in Hebrew, English, or both.

  3. Then it is up to you

    Go back, or wait a few seconds and continue. The app then stays open for a while: 15 minutes by day and 5 at night, and you can change both.

What you get

Gentle, and on your side.

A Little Light does not lecture you. It gives you a moment, and a few good words, right when you reach for your phone.

Real Torah

Over a thousand short passages, each with its source, from the library at Sefaria.

Help when you want it

Making things stricter works right away. Easing off waits ten minutes and asks you to confirm.

Stays on your phone

No account, no tracking, and the app cannot use the internet at all.
