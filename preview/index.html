מְעַט אוֹר

Not a replacement for your filter. A light beside it.

A Little Light is an Android app that works alongside the filter you already use. Before the apps you choose, it shows you a short passage of Torah. Then the choice is yours.

The problem

A filter blocks what is harmful. It cannot stop the scrolling.

Even with a good filter on, the phone can still take an evening. One allowed app leads to the next. Late at night, in bed, in the dark, it is hardest to put down.

The answer

A little light, right before the app opens.

The Tanya teaches that a little light pushes away a great deal of darkness, and the darkness gives way by itself. That is the idea behind the app. Before an app you chose opens, it puts a few lines of Torah in front of you. Then you decide.

Where the name comes from

A little light pushes away a great deal of darkness.

A few lines of Torah, before the app opens. A little light.

How it works

A pause, a passage, then your choice.

  1. Pick the apps that pull at you

    For each app, you decide: open as usual, a moment of Torah first, or closed.

  2. Torah comes first

    When you open one of those apps, a passage of Torah fills the screen before it, in Hebrew, English, or both.

  3. Then it is up to you

    Go back, or wait a few seconds and continue. The app then stays open for a while: 15 minutes by day and 5 at night, and you can change both.

What you get

Gentle, and on your side.

It does not lecture you. It gives you a few good words when you reach for your phone.

Real Torah

Over a thousand short passages, each with its source, from the library at Sefaria.

Help when you want it

Making things stricter works right away. Easing off waits ten minutes and asks you to confirm.

Stays on your phone

No account, no tracking, and the app cannot use the internet at all.

Our mission

A little light of Torah, beside the filter you trust.

To bring a few words of Torah to the moments the phone pulls hardest, and to leave the decision with you.
