Privacy policy

Privacy policy

The short version: A Little Light keeps everything on your phone. It has no internet permission, no account and no analytics.

Applies to A Little Light for Android, version 3.0.24. Last updated November 23, 2026.

What the app collects

Nothing leaves your phone. The app does not ask Android for internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information, and we have no servers that hold any.

What stays on your phone

  • Your choices: which apps open as usual, show a passage first, or stay closed; your websites; how long the wait is; how long Continue lasts; and your night hours.
  • If you add them: your own line, and the name and number of one person to call or text from Sanctuary.
  • Your starred passages and your groups.

This information is stored only inside the app on your phone. Android backups and device-to-device transfer are turned off for it. You can delete your line and your contact from Setup & privacy, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

Permissions and why

  • Accessibility service. To notice which app comes to the front and, in a web browser, the address of the page, so a passage can appear first and your website choices can work. This access lets the app read window content; it is used only for that purpose, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Notifications. Only to tell you if Android has stopped A Little Light.
  • Usage access (optional, granted by you in Android settings). Only to sort your app list by most used. It is read on the phone only.
  • Installed apps list. So the Apps screen can show every app on your phone and you can choose a rule for each one.
  • Run at startup. So A Little Light's checks pick up again after the phone restarts.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
